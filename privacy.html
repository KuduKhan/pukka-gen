Privacy notice

This notice describes how website enquiries are intended to work. The responsible legal entity, service-provider arrangements, retention period and complete compliance disclosures still require independent verification and legal review. It must not be treated as a finalized privacy policy.

What we collect

When you submit a project enquiry, the form requests your name, email address, selected service and project description. Please do not include passwords, sensitive records or confidential source code.

Why we use it

The submitted information is used to review your enquiry and reply to your message. We do not use this form to make automated decisions about individuals.

How it is delivered

If configured, the server forwards your enquiry through a transactional email provider to the Pukka Gen inbox. If delivery is unavailable, the website offers a direct email link instead of claiming the message arrived.

Retention and access

Enquiry information may be retained in the company mailbox and email delivery system for business correspondence. The exact retention schedule and service-provider terms remain subject to confirmation. Avoid sharing confidential or sensitive information through this public form.
